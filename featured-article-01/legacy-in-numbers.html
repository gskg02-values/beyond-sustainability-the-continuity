NATURE — IN ONE NUMBER

A legacy written long before civilisation
4.54 BILLION YEARS
Earth’s age

Nature’s laboratory has been running for roughly 4.54 billion years — long before humanity existed to name its experiments.

3.77 BILLION YEARS
Evidence of ancient life

Fossil evidence indicates that life was already present on Earth at least 3.77 billion years ago — survival has a history far older than civilisation.

2.4 BILLION YEARS
The atmosphere changed

Around 2.4 billion years ago, the Great Oxidation Event transformed Earth’s atmosphere; biological activity helped reshape the planet itself.

71%
The living ocean’s domain

One global ocean covers about 71% of Earth’s surface — a planetary system continuously regulating climate, heat and life.

MILLIONS OF YEARS
Adaptation without a blueprint

Species have continuously responded to changing environments through evolution, behaviour, structure and ecological relationships — without a central designer.

∞ CONTINUITY
The greatest number of all

Nature does not measure success by what survives unchanged. Its greatest achievement is the ability of life to adapt, regenerate and continue.
