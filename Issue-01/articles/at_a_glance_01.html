The Unspoken Insight

Tamil Nadu’s estimated annual drinking water requirement is only 87.6 GL.

Based on the engineering assumptions presented in this article, three strategically located 100 MLD desalination plants, each supported by three dedicated 15 MW offshore wind turbines, could theoretically produce 109.5 GL of drinking water per year — approximately 25% above the estimated statewide drinking water requirement.

The concept demonstrates that the ocean can become a dependable freshwater source while renewable offshore wind provides the energy needed to convert seawater into safe drinking water, reducing reliance on fossil-fuel electricity.
