💡 "Did You Know"
💥 The Ballistic Sandbox Tree (Forests & Trees)
  • Fact: The Hura crepitans tree disperses its seeds by allowing its pumpkin-shaped fruit capsules to violently explode, launching seeds at 150 mph up to 100 feet away.
  • Note: This aggressive, built-in mechanical system ensures the seeds escape the parent tree's shadow, finding the open sunlight necessary to establish their own foundation.
