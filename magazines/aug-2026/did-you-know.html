Fun Facts: Forests

  • 🌲 Forests can make their own rain. Trees release water vapor that helps form clouds and rainfall.
  • 🍄 The largest living organism on Earth is a fungus. Growing beneath a forest, it covers several square kilometers.
  • 🕸️ Trees share nutrients underground. They use fungal networks often called the "Wood Wide Web."
  • 🔥 Some pine cones open only after a forest fire. Fire helps the next generation of trees begin growing.
  • 🌱 A teaspoon of healthy forest soil contains billions of microorganisms, many still unknown to science.
